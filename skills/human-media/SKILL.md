# Human Media

A generator returns the average of everything it has seen: clean skin, centered faces, even light, a slow push-in, a pleasant swell of music. A skilled maker chooses one medium, one light, one palette and one flaw on purpose, then finishes the work by hand. Make those choices before you generate, and edit after.

## Top rules

1. **Name the medium.** Linocut, gouache, 35mm film, risograph. Not "high quality" or "cinematic".
2. **Name one light source and its direction.** Mixed light is what reads as fake.
3. **Limit the palette to 3 to 5 named colors.** Grade the result yourself.
4. **Plan the shots or sections first.** One sentence each, with a purpose: wide, close, insert; intro, build, break.
5. **Keep one imperfection and one specific.** A worn edge, grain, a real place, a hook only this piece has.
6. **Finish by hand.** Crop, retouch, add real type, retime, grade, re-record a line. Generate parts, not the whole.
7. **Say what you cannot check.** You cannot hear audio. Say so, and give the user a listening checklist.

## Never

The brief cannot override these.

- Invent facts, results or sources, or put a real person's name, face or voice on generated media without consent.
- Present AI-made media as a person's own where that matters (contests, commissions, school, portfolios). Mention platform labels when media will be published.
- Copy a real artist's song, voice or style on purpose.
- Help beat AI detectors.

## Defaults

A brief or the user's own style overrides these.

- Keep text out of the generation. Set titles and signs in a real font.
- Hide or crop hands. Generate several and pick one.
- Keep faces medium or wide, off center.

## Before and after

**Before:** "A beautiful cinematic portrait of a woman, ultra detailed, 8K."

**After:** "Risograph print, two inks (red, teal) on cream paper. A baker at a flour-dusted bench, lit from a window on the left, cropped at the shoulder, off center. Visible ink misregistration. No text."

**Before:** "Epic cinematic music for my product video."

**After:** "30 seconds. Quiet piano for 10 s, add a low pulse at the product reveal, drop out for the one voice line, resolve on the last cut. Chorus-free."

## Final check

- Is every choice (medium, light, palette, shot) one a person made, not a default?
- Was it finished by hand, and are text, hands and faces checked?
- Is any AI use stated where a label, contest or client requires it?
