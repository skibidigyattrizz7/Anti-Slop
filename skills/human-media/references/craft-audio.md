# Audio craft

Evidence status: thin. The audio research pass was partial. Only the points below are sourced. Everything else is labelled unverified or left out. I cannot hear audio, so this file gives direction and a listening checklist, not a verdict.

## Music

Sourced:
- A detector paper (SONICS, arXiv 2408.14080, v4 2025-02-25) says synthetic songs are identified by patterns across the whole song, not short frames. The craft reading is an inference: develop the piece across its length.
- Spotify (2025-09-25) adopted DDEX AI credits so AI use in vocals, instrumentation or post-production can be disclosed, and bans unauthorized voice clones. A missing credit does not mean no AI was used.

Craft moves (practice, not sourced in the research notes):
- Write a real structure: intro, build, break, return. Add material, remove layers, change density.
- Vary dynamics and leave space. Silence is a choice.
- Give the piece one hook only it has.
- Replace or re-record parts with live or hand-played ones where you can. Re-arrange in a DAW.
- Do not copy a real artist's song, melody or voice.

## Voiceover and narration

Sourced:
- Google Cloud TTS SSML docs: `<prosody>`, `<emphasis>` and `<break>` control pitch, rate, volume, emphasis and pauses. Wrap whole sentences, not single words, to avoid odd pauses. The docs are undated, so check them against the engine you use.

Craft moves (practice, not sourced):
- Write for the ear: short sentences, contractions, one point per line.
- Mark where the pauses and stresses go. Markup helps only where the writer decided them.
- Generate line by line, and vary speed. Re-generate a line that is flat.
- Check names and numbers for pronunciation. Spell them phonetically in the script.
- Read it aloud yourself first.
- Land pauses on cuts when it goes under video. Music drops under speech.

## Sound design and mix

Practice only, no source retrieved:
- Layer real recordings: room tone, foley, one memorable sound per scene.
- Use a reference track in the same genre to set level and tone.
- Subtitles break at phrases, in one font and one position.

## Disclosure and rights

- Tell the user to check the current AI-disclosure rules of the platform, distributor or contest. No verified rule beyond Spotify's DDEX credits was found for Audible, ACX or YouTube.
- Voice clones need the speaker's consent. Do not clone a real person's voice without it.
- Describe what was AI-made (vocals, instruments, post-production), not just yes or no.

## Listening checklist for the user

- Does the track change at least three times?
- Is there a breath or pause in every voice line, and is stress on the right word?
- Are names and numbers said right?
- Do level changes follow the cut?
