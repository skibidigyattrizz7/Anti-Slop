# Own voice tells (dated)

last_verified: 2026-11-19 against the owner's original skill. No outside study is cited here.
Re-check: stock lines every 6 months, everything after a major model release.

A tell is a reason to rework a line, never proof of who wrote it. One tell means nothing. Several together mean the draft has no person in it.

## Structure

| Tell | Fix |
|---|---|
| A trait claimed with no scene ("I am a resilient leader") | Ask for the moment, then write the moment |
| Polished tone that never slips, from the first line to the last | Let one plain, slightly rough line stay |
| Vocabulary the person never uses elsewhere | Swap in words from their sample or chat |
| Every sentence the same length | Add a short one after a long one |
| Ends on a moral or lesson | End on a detail, decision or next step |
| Experiences that could belong to anyone ("a challenging project") | Ask for the name, number or place |

## Stock lines

- "This experience shaped who I am today."
- "Taught me the value of perseverance and teamwork."
- "Passionate team player", "results-driven", "responsible for".
- "I hope this email finds you well." "I am reaching out to touch base."
- "I have always been passionate about..."
- "Ever since I was young, I have dreamed of..."
- Grand claims about changing the world.

## Word level

Count these only in clusters of three or more: journey, passion, impactful, driven, dynamic, tapestry, resilient, multifaceted. Each one is a prompt to ask what actually happened.

## Guards

- Formal, simple or non-native English is not AI writing. Do not "fix" it toward native polish.
- A line the user wrote and wants to keep stays. Mention the concern once, then respect the choice.
- Do not suggest ways to evade AI detectors. If the rules ban AI help, offer coaching only.
- English lists do not transfer to other languages.
