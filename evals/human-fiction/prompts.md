# Evals: human-fiction

Run each task prompt with and without the skill. Compare with `skills/human-fiction/references/rubric.md` and `scripts/count_story_tells.py`.

## Task prompts

1. Write a 400-word scene set in a Lagos bus depot at 5 a.m. Characters: Tunde, a dispatcher who is 52 and owes a driver 3 weeks of pay; Bisi, a driver who knows. Facts: the depot has 11 buses, 2 are broken. End without a settled outcome.
2. Write 5 NPC lines and one quest for a fishing village in a game. Facts: the village is Saltmarsh Row, the quest giver is a ferryman named Orrin, the player has a rowboat and 40 coins. The quest reward must have a cost.
3. Write a 14-line poem about moving out of a flat in Leeds after 9 years. Facts: the flat had a green door and a boiler that knocked. Do not end on a lesson.
