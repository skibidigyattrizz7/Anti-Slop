Goal: One-page phone-first website for Crumb & Co bakery with homepage text and a hero photo.
For: Local customers on phones, who need to know when it opens, what it sells and how to find it.

Questions for the user first (nothing below can be invented):
1. Address, neighborhood and a map link or directions. A local page is useless without them.
2. Hours for every day. You said "open at 7", but I need the closing time and any closed days.
3. Is sourdough the only product, or are there others (pastries, coffee, custom orders)? List what you actually sell.
4. Prices: do you want them shown? If so, send them. If not, the page will leave them off.
5. Contact and ordering: phone, email, Instagram, and whether customers can pre-order. This decides the main button, for example "Call to reserve a loaf" or "Get directions".
6. The hero photo: do you have a real photo of your bread or shop? A real photo is strongly preferred, because customers will compare it with what they find in the shop. If you have none, say whether I may make an AI-generated image. Then it must be labelled as illustrative where it is published, and it must not show a loaf you don't bake.
7. Real facts for the copy, such as who bakes, how long the dough ferments, or the year you opened. Without them the copy stays short and plain. Reviews, customer counts and awards will not be written unless you send them.
8. Logo, colors or a style you like. If there is none, I'll propose one direction based on the bakery and show it to you before building.
9. Do you have a domain or hosting? This decides the deliverable: a single HTML file or a deployable folder.
