**Moving lunch from 12:00 to 12:30: summary for the principal**

**Recommendation:** Approve 12:30 as a trial, on one condition: confirm how many students would eat in a single period. If that number is 300 or fewer, run one 12:30 lunch. If it is more than 300, split lunch into two sittings instead of a full switch. After the trial, repeat the survey and decide whether to keep the change. Suggested trial length is four to six weeks; that figure is my suggestion, not drawn from the data.

**Why**
- **Students lean toward 12:30.** In the October 2026 survey, 410 students answered and 58% prefer 12:30. That is about 238 of 410 (0.58 × 410 = 237.8). The other 42% did not choose 12:30; the data given does not say what they chose.
- **12:30 fits the schedule.** Two classes end at 12:25, so a 12:30 lunch starts after they finish. Those classes would overlap a 12:00 lunch if they run through 12:00, which the data given does not state. Start times were not provided.
- **The cafeteria is the limit.** It seats 300. Any single period with more than 300 students cannot all sit at once.

**What is not known**
- Total enrollment, so we cannot say whether one 12:30 period stays under 300 seats.
- Whether the 410 respondents represent the whole school. The 58% is a share of respondents only.

**Decision needed:** whether to approve the trial, once enrollment per period is confirmed.

**Sources**
All supplied by the requester; none opened or independently verified.
1. School survey, October 2026: 410 student respondents, 58% prefer 12:30.
2. Cafeteria capacity: 300 seats.
3. Class schedule: two classes end at 12:25.
