Join the Elm Road Community Garden: 12 New Raised Beds Are Coming!

Good news for green thumbs. The Elm Road garden is expanding with 12 brand-new raised beds, giving more neighbors a place to grow vegetables, herbs, and flowers this season.

Sign-ups open on 3 March. Each bed costs just $20 per season, making fresh, homegrown produce affordable for everyone.

Demand is high. Last year's waiting list reached 31 names, so we encourage you to mark your calendar and sign up early once registration opens. Beds are expected to fill quickly.

Whether you're a seasoned gardener or a first-timer eager to dig in, there's a bed waiting for you. Watch this newsletter and our community bulletin board for details on how to register.

See you in the garden!
