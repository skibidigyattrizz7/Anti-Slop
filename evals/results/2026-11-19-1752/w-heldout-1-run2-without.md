Exciting news for our community! The garden on Elm Road is growing, and we're adding 12 brand-new raised beds this season. Whether you're an experienced gardener or have never planted a seed, there's a place for you to grow your own vegetables, herbs, and flowers right in the heart of our neighbourhood. Working side by side is a wonderful way to meet neighbours, share tips, and enjoy the satisfaction of harvesting food you've nurtured yourself.

Sign-ups open on 3 March, and we expect strong demand. Last year our waiting list reached 31 names, so early registration is the best way to secure a bed. Each bed costs just $20 per season, making fresh, homegrown produce affordable for everyone.

Mark 3 March on your calendar, tell your friends and family about it, and get ready to dig in together. We can't wait to welcome you to the garden this growing season!
