Big news for our community! The garden on Elm Road is growing, with 12 brand-new raised beds ready for planting this season. Sign-ups open 3 March, and beds cost just 20 dollars for the full season.

Demand is high. Last year's waiting list reached 31 names, so we encourage you to sign up as soon as registration opens. Early applicants have the best chance of securing a bed. Spots are limited, so don't wait until the last minute.

Whether you're a seasoned gardener or just starting out, there's room for you here. Fresh vegetables, colorful flowers, and friendly neighbors await. Bring your gloves, your enthusiasm, and a friend or two. Our volunteers will be on hand to help you get started.

Mark your calendar for 3 March, and help us turn these new beds into a thriving green space for everyone on Elm Road. Together, we can grow something wonderful.
