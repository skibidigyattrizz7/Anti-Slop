**Summary: Moving Lunch from 12:00 to 12:30**

**Question:** Should the school move lunch from 12:00 to 12:30?

**What we know**

- **Student preference:** In the school's October 2026 survey, 410 students responded. Of these, 58% (about 238 students) prefer a 12:30 lunch. The survey does not break down the other 42% by preference, so we cannot tell how many want 12:00 and how many had no preference.
- **Cafeteria capacity:** The cafeteria seats 300. Total enrollment is not in the survey data, so we cannot yet say whether all students can eat in one sitting at either time.
- **Class schedule:** Two classes end at 12:25. Under the current 12:00 lunch, students in those classes arrive after lunch has started. A 12:30 lunch would let them eat without rushing or missing the period.

**Analysis**

A 12:30 lunch has a clear benefit for the two classes that end at 12:25, and a majority of surveyed students prefer it. The preference is moderate, not overwhelming, and the survey measures what students want, not whether the change would work in practice. The move also does nothing to fix capacity. If enrollment exceeds 300, the school will need staggered seating at either time.

**Recommendation**

Move lunch to 12:30 as a one-term trial, on two conditions:

1. **Confirm seating capacity.** Check total enrollment against the 300 seats. If more students eat than seats allow, split lunch into two waves (for example, 12:30 and 12:45) before making the change.
2. **Check supervision and the afternoon schedule.** Confirm that staff can cover the cafeteria at 12:30 and that afternoon classes and activities are not disrupted.

At the end of the trial, survey students and staff again and compare attendance at lunch, cafeteria congestion, and any effect on afternoon classes before deciding whether to keep 12:30 permanently.
