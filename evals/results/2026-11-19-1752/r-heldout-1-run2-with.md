**Lunch at 12:30 instead of 12:00: summary for the principal**
*Prepared 19 Nov 2026*

**Recommendation:** Move lunch to 12:30 for a two-week trial, but only if the cafeteria can seat everyone who eats lunch in one sitting. If it cannot, run two staggered sittings rather than moving the whole school at once.

**What the survey shows.** Your survey (October 2026, 410 student responses) found that 58% prefer 12:30. That is about 240 of 410 students (58% × 410 = 237.8, rounded). The survey does not record the total school roll or the response rate, so we do not know whether the 410 respondents represent all students. Preferring a time is also not the same as eating at that time.

**Capacity.** The cafeteria seats 300. Whether the move works depends on how many students eat lunch in one sitting, which the survey does not give. At 300 or fewer, 12:30 fits in one sitting. Above 300, one sitting fails at either time, so the change to 12:30 does not solve the problem.

**Class timing.** Two classes end at 12:25. A 12:30 lunch leaves those students five minutes to reach the cafeteria, which is tight. The number of students in those two classes is not known. Check whether those classes can be released a few minutes early.

**Unknowns.** Total enrolment and lunch headcount; the survey's response rate and how respondents were chosen; what the 42% who did not choose 12:30 prefer; whether the preference holds among students who buy school lunch.

**Decision and owner.** The principal approves the two-week trial once the headcount and the 12:25 release question are checked. If lunch headcount is above 300, or the two classes cannot reach the cafeteria in time, do not move the whole school to 12:30.

**Sources**
- Your school's lunch-time survey, October 2026, 410 student responses. Figures as you reported them; not independently checked. No link.
- Cafeteria capacity (300 seats) and class end time (12:25, two classes). School facts you supplied; not checked against a timetable or floor plan.
