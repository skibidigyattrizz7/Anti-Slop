**Lunch at 12:30: recommendation for the principal**

**Recommendation:** Trial the 12:30 lunch for one term, on condition that the three checks below are settled first. Majority student preference supports the change, but the cafeteria's seat count and the 12:25 class endings have to work at 12:30.

**What students want:** 58% of the 410 students who answered prefer 12:30, which is about 240 students (58% × 410 = 237.8). That is a majority of respondents, not a strong mandate. We do not know the school's total enrolment, so we cannot say what share of all students this represents.

**Capacity:** The cafeteria seats 300. That is 73% of the 410 respondents (300 ÷ 410 = 0.73). If everyone eats in one sitting, about 110 students would have no seat. The seat count is the same at 12:00 and 12:30, so changing the time does not fix it. A staggered or two-sitting lunch would.

**Class timing:** Two classes end at 12:25. A 12:30 start gives those students five minutes to reach the cafeteria. Whether that is enough is not known.

**Trial and review:** Run 12:30 for one term. Record queue length, seats taken and the number of students eating school lunch. Lunch uptake has not been measured, so there is no baseline; record it before the change starts.

**What would change the recommendation:** If total enrolment means far more than 300 students need to eat at once, or the two classes cannot be released at 12:25, keep 12:00 or introduce two sittings before moving.

**Owner:** The principal. The brief gave no decision date, so the decision should be made before the timetable change is due.

**Unknown, named once:** total enrolment; the survey's exact question wording and response rate; whether the 300 seats can serve all lunch-eaters in one sitting; whether the two classes can be released earlier.
