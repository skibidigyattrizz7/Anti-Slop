# Summary: Moving Lunch from 12:00 to 12:30

**Recommendation:** Approve the move to 12:30 on a one-term trial, conditional on confirming that the cafeteria can seat each lunch shift at the new time.

**Why consider the change**
- **Student preference:** In the October 2026 survey, 58% of the 410 respondents (about 238 students) preferred 12:30. The remaining 42% (about 172 students) did not, so the preference is a clear majority but not an overwhelming one.
- **Class conflict:** Two classes end at 12:25. With lunch at 12:00, students in those classes either miss lunch or eat late. A 12:30 start lets them reach the cafeteria after their class ends.

**Constraints and risks**
- **Seating:** The cafeteria seats 300. If the whole student body eats in one sitting, seating will be short. Enrollment is not in the survey, so we cannot confirm this from the data. Staggered shifts would be needed if enrollment exceeds 300.
- **Afternoon schedule:** A 12:30 lunch may shorten the afternoon or affect instructional time. This has not been checked.
- **Sample reliability:** The survey covers 410 respondents. The response rate and whether the sample was representative are not reported. At 95% confidence, the margin of error for 58% is roughly ±5 points, so the true share is likely between about 53% and 63%. Results from a self-selected survey may differ from those of the full student body.

**Next steps**
1. Confirm total enrollment and how many students would eat in each shift at 12:30.
2. Check the afternoon timetable for lost instructional time.
3. Run the 12:30 lunch for one term, then gather feedback from students, staff, and cafeteria supervisors before deciding whether to keep it.

**Sources**
- School survey on lunch timing, October 2026 (410 student respondents). Figures used: 58% prefer 12:30; cafeteria capacity of 300 seats; two classes ending at 12:25.
- Margin of error calculated from the survey figures (standard 95% confidence formula, assuming a random sample). No external sources were consulted.
