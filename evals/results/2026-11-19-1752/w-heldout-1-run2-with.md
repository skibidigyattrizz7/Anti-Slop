Elm Road is getting bigger. The community garden is adding 12 new raised beds this season, so there will be more room for growers who have been waiting.

For $20 for the season, you get a raised bed of your own to plant as you like. A raised bed sits above the ground, so you control the soil you grow in.

Sign-ups open on 3 March, so mark the date. Last year the waiting list had 31 names, more than the new beds can cover. If you want a bed, don't leave sign-up until later.

New growers are welcome, and one bed is a manageable first plot. Plan what you want to grow now, so you can start the day your bed is ready. Mark the date and tell a neighbor who might want a bed.
