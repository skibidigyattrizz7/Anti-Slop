# Lunch at 12:30 instead of 12:00

**Recommendation:** Move lunch to 12:30 as a one-term trial, but only once the cafeteria can seat every student who will eat at that time. The survey supports the change. It does not yet show that the 300 seats are enough.

**What the evidence shows**

- **Preference:** 58% of the 410 students who answered prefer 12:30 (58% × 410 = 237.8, so about 240 students). The other 42% (42% × 410 = 172.2, so about 170) do not. Source: your school survey, October 2026. I have not checked it against the roster, and the response rate is unknown.
- **Class timing:** Two classes end at 12:25. A 12:00 lunch falls while they are still in session, so their students miss it. A 12:30 start lets them eat with everyone else. This is my reading of the class times. I do not know how many students are in those two classes.
- **Capacity:** The cafeteria seats 300. Whether one sitting at 12:30 fits depends on how many students eat lunch at school, which is not in the facts I have. Not measured.

**Open questions**

1. How many students eat lunch at school? If more than 300 need seats, a 12:30 start needs two sittings.
2. Does a later lunch change the afternoon timetable or the end of the school day?
3. Did the survey ask one clear question, and who was asked?

**Decision needed:** Approve a one-term trial at 12:30 from the start of next term. Record how many students eat in the first two weeks, then compare it with the 300 seats. Owner: the principal. Cost: not measured. Change the recommendation if more than 300 students eat at once, or if the afternoon timetable cannot absorb the shift.
