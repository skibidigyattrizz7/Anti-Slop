**To: Principal**
**Re: Moving lunch from 12:00 to 12:30**
**Date: 19 November 2026**

**Summary**

The school surveyed students in October 2026. Of the 410 who responded, 58 percent (about 238 students) prefer a 12:30 lunch, and 42 percent did not. The cafeteria seats 300. Two classes end at 12:25, so under the current 12:00 start, those students would reach lunch partway through the period.

**Findings**

- **Student preference:** A majority favors 12:30, but the margin is modest, and the 42 percent who did not choose it should not be ignored.
- **Schedule fit:** A 12:30 start means the two classes ending at 12:25 can reach the cafeteria without missing the start of lunch. A 12:00 start does not allow this.
- **Capacity:** The survey does not report total enrollment. If more than 300 students eat lunch, a single seating at either time will not fit everyone, and the change alone will not solve that.
- **Survey limits:** The results reflect the 410 students who responded, not necessarily the whole student body. Response rates and whether non-respondents differ are unknown.

**Recommendation**

Move lunch to 12:30, on a one-term trial, under two conditions:

1. Confirm total lunch attendance. If it exceeds 300, use two staggered seatings (for example, 12:30 and 12:45) so that no group is over capacity.
2. Check the afternoon schedule to confirm that moving lunch does not shorten any class or cause a conflict with other timetabled activities.

At the end of the trial, reissue a short survey to students and staff and confirm the change before making it permanent.

**Sources**

- School student survey, October 2026 (n = 410 respondents): preference for lunch at 12:30 (58 percent), with the remaining 42 percent not preferring that time.
- School facilities data: cafeteria seating capacity of 300.
- School timetable data: two classes ending at 12:25.

No external sources were provided, so none are cited. Total enrollment and the full timetable should be attached before a final decision.
