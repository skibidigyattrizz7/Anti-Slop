Nothing is wrong with you. Getting out of breath after two minutes usually means you're running faster than your current fitness supports, and most beginners start too fast because the first minute feels easy.

Slow down until you can talk in short sentences while you run. Breathing hard means the effort is too high for now, not that your body can't do it.

Try intervals: jog for 1 minute, walk for 2, and repeat 6 times, 3 days a week. Each week, make the jog a bit longer and the walk a bit shorter. After a few weeks, running for 2 minutes will feel much easier.

See a doctor if you get chest pain, dizziness, or fainting, or if you wheeze or feel tightness in your chest, especially when resting or doing light activity. Asthma is common and treatable, and it can feel like being out of shape.
