Nothing is necessarily wrong with you. Most people who start running get out of breath within a couple of minutes, because your body hasn't built up the stamina for it yet. Running at 16 is a great time to start.

Some things that usually help:

- **Run slower than feels right.** You should be able to talk in full sentences. If you can't, slow down or walk.
- **Use intervals.** Try 1 minute of easy jogging, then 2 minutes of walking, and repeat for 20 to 30 minutes. Over a few weeks, shift toward longer jogging stretches and shorter walks.
- **Run 3 times a week** with a rest day between sessions. Your fitness improves while you recover, so you don't need to go hard every day.
- **Breathe steadily.** Try to breathe in and out in a rhythm you can keep up, through your nose or mouth, whichever feels natural.
- **Warm up** with a 5-minute brisk walk, and finish with a short walk to cool down.
- **Be patient.** Most people notice a real difference within 3 to 4 weeks.

See a doctor if you get chest pain or tightness, dizziness or fainting, wheezing or a cough that doesn't go away, or breathlessness that stays severe even when you're resting. Also mention it if anyone in your family has had heart problems at a young age. These are uncommon, but they're worth checking.

If none of those apply and you're just out of shape, keep going. In a few weeks, two minutes will feel a lot easier.
