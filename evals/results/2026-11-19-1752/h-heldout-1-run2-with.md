Nothing is necessarily wrong with you. Most people who start running get out of breath within a couple of minutes, because their heart and lungs aren't trained for it yet, and that improves over a few weeks.

Start with intervals instead of trying to run straight through. Jog for 1 minute, walk for 2, and repeat 6 to 8 times, three days a week. Keep the jogging pace slow enough that you could say a full sentence without gasping. Going too fast at the start is the usual reason people get breathless quickly and quit. Each week, shorten the walk breaks or lengthen the jogs, and within a month or two you should be running 10 to 20 minutes without stopping.

See a doctor before you push harder if you get chest pain or tightness, dizziness, fainting, or pain spreading to your arm or jaw. Also get checked if you wheeze or cough when you exercise, since exercise-induced asthma is common and treatable.
