Nothing is necessarily wrong with you. Getting out of breath after two minutes is very common when you're starting out. Breathing hard is your heart and lungs adjusting to new demands, and fitness usually improves within a few weeks of consistent practice.

Most beginners run too fast. A useful check is the talk test: you should be able to say a short sentence comfortably while running. If you can't, slow down, even if it feels silly.

Try this:
- **Run/walk intervals:** Jog for 1 minute, then walk for 1 to 2 minutes. Repeat for 15 to 20 minutes. Over time, make the running parts longer and the walking parts shorter.
- **Run 3 times a week** with a rest day between sessions.
- **Warm up** with 5 minutes of brisk walking before you start.
- **Breathe steadily**, using a rhythm such as breathing in for two steps and out for two.
- **Be patient.** Most people can run 20 to 30 minutes continuously within about 6 to 10 weeks.

See a doctor, and tell a parent or guardian, if you notice any of these:
- Chest pain or tightness, or pain spreading to your arm, jaw, or neck
- Fainting, near-fainting, or dizziness
- Wheezing or coughing that doesn't go away, especially if you have asthma or have been told you have it
- Breathlessness that is severe even when you walk or sit
- A racing or irregular heartbeat that doesn't settle down
- A family history of heart problems or sudden death at a young age

If none of these apply, you're most likely just out of shape, and that's very fixable. Good luck with it.
