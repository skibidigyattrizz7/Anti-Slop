Twelve new raised beds are coming to the community garden on Elm Road this season. Sign-ups open on 3 March, and each bed costs $20 for the season.

Demand is high. Last year the waiting list reached 31 names, more than twice the number of beds we are adding. The new beds will not go around to everyone who asked last year, so if you want one, plan to sign up on the first day.

The cost is $20 for the whole season, so a bed is an easy yes if you have been thinking about growing your own. Mark 3 March now, and set a reminder for the day sign-ups open. [detail needed: where to sign up]

If you have never grown anything, a raised bed is a good place to start. The soil sits above the ground, so you can reach every plant without bending far.
