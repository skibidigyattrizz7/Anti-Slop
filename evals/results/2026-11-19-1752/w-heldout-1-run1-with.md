The Elm Road community garden is adding 12 new raised beds this season. Sign-ups open on 3 March. Each bed costs $20 for the season.

Demand is high. Last year the waiting list reached 31 names, more than twice the number of new beds, so 12 beds will not cover everyone. If you want one, be ready on 3 March.

Twenty dollars for a season is a small cost for fresh vegetables, herbs or flowers you grew yourself, and a bed gives you a regular reason to get outside.

If you have never grown anything before, this is a good year to start. Bring a friend along for the ride.
