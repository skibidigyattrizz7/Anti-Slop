Nothing is necessarily wrong with you. Getting out of breath after two minutes is very common when you're starting out, and most people who run regularly were once in your position. Your breathing is limited by how fit your heart, lungs, and leg muscles are right now, and that improves with consistent practice.

Some things that help:

- **Run slower than feels necessary.** You should be able to talk in full sentences. If you can't, slow down or walk.
- **Use intervals.** Try 1 minute of easy jogging, then 2 minutes of walking, and repeat for 20 to 30 minutes. Over a few weeks, gradually make the running parts longer and the walking parts shorter.
- **Run 3 times a week** with a rest day between sessions. Your body adapts during recovery, not during the run itself.
- **Warm up** with 5 minutes of walking before you start.
- **Breathe steadily.** Try a rhythm such as breathing in for 2 steps and out for 2 steps. Don't worry about perfect form.
- **Be patient.** Most people notice real improvement within 3 to 6 weeks.

See a doctor before you push harder if you ever get chest pain or pressure, faint or nearly faint, feel dizzy or your heart races or pounds in a way that worries you, or have wheezing or a cough that doesn't go away. Those symptoms are uncommon in healthy teens, but they're worth checking, especially if you've had them before.
