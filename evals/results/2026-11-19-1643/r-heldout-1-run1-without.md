**Summary: Moving Lunch from 12:00 to 12:30**

**Question:** Should the school move lunch from 12:00 to 12:30?

**What we know**
- An October 2026 survey of students drew 410 responses. 58% (about 238 students) prefer 12:30, and about 42% (about 172) do not.
- The cafeteria seats 300. Any single lunch period larger than that cannot be seated at once.
- Two classes end at 12:25. A 12:30 lunch would let their students eat right after class. At 12:00, those classes are likely still in session, so their students would have no lunch period.

**What we don't know**
- Total enrollment. If more than 300 students would eat in one period, a single 12:30 lunch will not fit in the cafeteria.
- Whether the 410 respondents represent the whole student body. The 58% figure is a share of respondents, not a confirmed school-wide figure.

**Recommendation**
Move lunch to 12:30 on a trial basis for four to six weeks, but only after confirming that the number of students eating in one period is 300 or fewer. If enrollment exceeds 300, split lunch into two staggered periods, such as 12:00 for some grades and 12:30 for others, instead of a full switch. At the end of the trial, run a short follow-up survey and decide whether to make the change permanent.

**Sources**
1. School lunch-preference survey, October 2026, 410 student respondents (school's own data; raw responses and enrollment were not reviewed for this summary).
2. Cafeteria capacity of 300 seats (figure as provided by the school).
3. Class schedule showing two classes ending at 12:25 (as provided by the school).

No external sources were consulted.
