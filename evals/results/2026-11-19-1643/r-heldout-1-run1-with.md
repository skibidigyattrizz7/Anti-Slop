# Lunch at 12:30 instead of 12:00: summary for the principal

**Recommendation:** Move lunch to 12:30 as a one-term trial, provided the cafeteria can seat every student who eats lunch at that time, in one sitting or two. The known facts favour 12:30. The one thing they do not settle is whether the cafeteria has room.

**Questions to answer before deciding**
1. How many students eat school lunch, and how many sittings does the cafeteria run? The 300 seats cannot be checked against demand until this is known.
2. Did the survey reach all students, or only some? 410 responses is a count, not a response rate, so the school-wide view is unknown.
3. Does a 12:30 lunch change the afternoon start for any group? This needs the timetable.

**What the survey shows** (your data: school lunch survey, October 2026, 410 student responses; I have not verified these figures independently)
- **58% preferred 12:30**, which is about 240 of the 410 respondents (0.58 × 410 = 237.8, rounded). This is a self-reported preference from one survey. It shows the views of those who answered, not of the whole school.
- **The cafeteria seats 300.** That is fewer than the 410 who answered, but respondents are not the lunch population, so this alone does not show a capacity problem.
- **Two classes end at 12:25.** At 12:00, lunch starts while those students are still in class. At 12:30, it starts after they finish.

**Why 12:30**
The majority preference and the 12:25 finish point the same way. A later lunch also means no class ends during lunch.

**What would change the recommendation**
- If daily lunch numbers exceed 300 at 12:30 and a second sitting is not possible, keep 12:00 or stagger lunch.
- If the 58% comes mainly from one year group, or the response rate is low, give the preference less weight and survey again before deciding.

**Next step:** Owner: principal. Get the lunch headcount and sitting count, then decide before the next timetable is set.
