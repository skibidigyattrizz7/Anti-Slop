Nothing is wrong with you. Almost every beginner gets winded after a couple of minutes, because your heart and lungs get better at running only after a few weeks of regular practice. Being out of breath means you're working hard, not that something is broken.

Start slower than feels right. You should be able to speak in short sentences while you run. If you can't, slow down or walk. Try jogging for 1 minute, walking for 2, and repeating that 8 times, 3 days a week with rest days in between. Each week, make the jogs a bit longer or the walks a bit shorter. Most people can run continuously for 20 to 30 minutes within a couple of months of doing this.

See a doctor before you push harder if you get chest pain or tightness, feel faint or dizzy, have a racing heartbeat while resting, or have asthma and wheeze when you run. Those need checking. Breathlessness on its own is usually just fitness.
