Nothing is wrong with you. Being out of breath after two minutes is very common when you're starting out, because your lungs, heart, and leg muscles haven't adapted to running yet. Most people who start running feel the same way, and it improves within a few weeks if you keep at it.

Some things that help:

- **Run slower than feels right.** You should be able to talk in full sentences. If you can't, slow down or walk.
- **Use run-walk intervals.** Try 1 minute running and 1 minute walking, repeated 8 to 10 times. Over time, make the running parts longer and the walking parts shorter.
- **Run 3 times a week** with a rest day between sessions. Your body adapts during recovery, so consistency matters more than intensity.
- **Warm up** with 5 minutes of brisk walking before you run.
- **Track progress** by noticing how long you can run before you need to walk, not by speed.

See a doctor before you push harder if you get chest pain or pressure, dizziness, fainting, or unusual wheezing or coughing when you exercise, or if you have a heart condition or asthma you haven't talked about with a doctor. Those symptoms are worth getting checked, even though breathlessness alone usually just means you're getting fitter.
