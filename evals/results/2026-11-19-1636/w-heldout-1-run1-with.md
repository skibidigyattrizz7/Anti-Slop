The Elm Road community garden is adding 12 new raised beds this season. Sign-ups open on 3 March, so mark the date now.

A bed costs $20 for the season. That is a small price for a plot you can tend yourself. Raised beds also make the work easier on your back, which matters more the longer you garden.

Last year the waiting list had 31 names on it, more than twice the number of beds we are adding now. If you want one this year, sign up on the first day rather than hoping the list moves.

I think raised beds are the easiest way to start growing your own food, and a single season is a cheap way to find out whether you like it before committing to anything bigger.
