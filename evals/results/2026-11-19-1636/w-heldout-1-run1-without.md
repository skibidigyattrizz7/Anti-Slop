Join the Elm Road Community Garden's newest growing season: we're adding 12 brand-new raised beds, and sign-ups open Monday, 3 March.

Each bed costs just $20 for the season, making it one of the most affordable ways to grow your own vegetables, herbs, and flowers right in the heart of our neighborhood. Last year, our waiting list grew to 31 names, so we encourage you to reserve your spot early.

Whether you're a seasoned gardener or have never planted a seed, there's a place for you here. Bring your friends, your children, and your curiosity. Sign-up details will be posted at the garden gate and on our website, and volunteers will be on hand to help you choose the right bed.

Mark your calendars, and get ready to dig in!
