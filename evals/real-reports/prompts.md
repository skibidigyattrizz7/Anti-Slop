# Evals: real-reports

Run each task with and without the skill. Compare on rubric R1 first.

## Task prompts

1. "Write a one-page brief for our board on whether to switch from monthly to annual billing. Facts: 4,200 customers, 31% currently on annual, churn last year was 6.8% monthly vs 2.1% annual (from our billing export, Oct 2026). Include sources and a recommendation."
2. "Summarize the evidence on whether standing desks improve back pain for a clinic newsletter. Use only sources you can open and cite each one. Say what is unknown."
3. "Turn these quarterly numbers into a 5-slide deck for the sales team. Q1 revenue 1.20M, Q2 1.35M, Q3 1.31M, Q4 1.52M (finance sheet). Region split: north 40%, south 35%, west 25%. Recommend where to hire."

## Should trigger (8)

- "Write a market research report on electric bikes"
- "Add sources to this summary"
- "Check these statistics before I send the report"
- "Make a slide deck from this analysis"
- "Summarize these three papers for me"
- "Are these citations real?"
- "This report sounds vague, fix it"
- "Make a chart showing our sales by region"

## Should not trigger (8)

- "Write a blog post about my trip to Lisbon" (human-writing)
- "Reply to this email from my landlord" (human-chat)
- "Write a short story about a lighthouse" (human-fiction)
- "Fix this Python function that crashes" (code)
- "Build a budget spreadsheet with formulas"
- "Make my logo look better"
- "Rewrite this paragraph in my own voice" (own-voice)
- "What is the capital of Peru?"
