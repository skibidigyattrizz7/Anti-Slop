You're close. The mistake is in one sign.

When you move the -7 to the other side, you have to add 7 to both sides, not subtract it:

5x - 7 = 3x + 9
5x = 3x + 9 + 7
5x = 3x + 16

Now move the 3x over by subtracting 3x from both sides:

2x = 16
x = 8

Check it: 5(8) - 7 = 33, and 3(8) + 9 = 33. Both sides match, so x = 8.

If you got x = 1, you probably did 9 - 7 = 2 instead of 9 + 7 = 16. Watch the sign every time you move a number across the equals sign.
