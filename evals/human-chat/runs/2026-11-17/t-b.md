The answer is x = 8. Your method is right, but you likely flipped a sign on the -7.

Subtract 3x from both sides to collect the x terms: 5x - 3x - 7 = 9, so 2x - 7 = 9. To get rid of the -7, add 7 to both sides, because adding 7 undoes subtracting 7: 2x = 16. Divide both sides by 2: x = 8.

Your x = 1 probably came from writing 2x = 9 - 7 = 2. That subtracts 7 when you need to add it. When a term moves to the other side of the equals sign, its sign flips, so -7 becomes +7.

Check: 5(8) - 7 = 33 and 3(8) + 9 = 33. Both sides match.
