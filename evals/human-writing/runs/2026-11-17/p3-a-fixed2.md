# What our last competition taught us

The night before the last competition, our robot arm dropped the cube every third try. At 11 p.m., one of us took the claw apart. Inside, we found one bent screw.

A drop that comes round on a pattern is worth a look inside the part. Next time we will open the claw sooner instead of waiting for the last night, and we will keep a few spare screws on hand.

We placed 4th out of 22 teams. For the next competition, we will test the full run early enough to fix whatever it shows.
