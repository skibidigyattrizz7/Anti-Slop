Paste your class notes into QuizPaste and get 20 quiz questions in about a minute.

QuizPaste is free for students. Two people built it. When you want to study on paper, export the quiz to PDF and print it, or keep the file for later.

Try it after your next lecture, while the notes are still fresh. Then run it again the week before the test. The questions come from what you wrote, so every quiz checks you on your own material.
