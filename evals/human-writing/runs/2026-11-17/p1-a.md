Paste your class notes into QuizPaste and get 20 quiz questions in about a minute. It is free for students.

QuizPaste was built by a two-person team who wanted a faster way to test what they had just learned. Paste in a lecture, a chapter summary or a page of messy notes, and the tool writes questions from that material, so you can check your memory before the exam instead of after it.

When your quiz is ready, export it to PDF. Print it for a study group, save it for later, or work through it on paper without a screen. Try it with last week's notes and see how much you still remember.
