# What our last competition taught us

The night before the competition, the robot arm dropped the cube every third try. That is one failure in three, and it left us one night to find the cause.

At 11 p.m., one of our team members took the claw apart. The cause was a single bent screw. A part that small was enough to make the grip unreliable, and it had not been found until that night.

We placed 4th out of 22 teams.

Three lessons stand out. First, check the hardware before the final night, not during it. Second, when a robot fails in the same way each time, take the mechanism apart before you change the code. Third, take the robot apart early, while there is still time to fix what you find.

Our checklist for next time starts with the claw: every screw, every fastener and every moving joint. A bolt takes a minute to check. The night before a competition is a bad time to learn that it was loose.
