# What our last competition taught us

The night before the competition, the robot arm dropped the cube every third try.

At 11 p.m., one of us took the claw apart. Inside was one bent screw. A part that small was behind the drops.

We placed 4th out of 22 teams, and what we took from the weekend is mostly about that night.

Test the whole run the day before. A drop every third try only shows up when you run the arm over and over. Keep spare screws in the pit kit, since one bent screw was enough to cause the drops. And when the arm fails the same way again and again, open the claw early instead of tuning around it. We opened it at 11 p.m.

Next season we want to test earlier and check the hardware before we touch the code.
