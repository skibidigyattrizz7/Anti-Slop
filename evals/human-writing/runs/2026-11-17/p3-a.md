# What our last competition taught us

Going into the competition, we thought the robot was ready. It wasn't. The night before, the robot arm dropped the cube every third try, and no amount of adjusting made the problem go away.

At 11 p.m., one team member decided to take the claw apart piece by piece. Inside, they found one bent screw. Once it was fixed, the grip held steady, and we ran the course again until we felt confident in it.

We didn't get much sleep, but the night taught us three things. First, test the full run the day before, not just a quick check. Second, keep spare fasteners in the pit kit, because one small part can stop the whole machine. Third, when something breaks, stop guessing and inspect each part in order. Searching methodically found the problem faster than guessing ever did.

In the end, we placed 4th out of 22 teams. We're proud of that finish, and we know how close it came to going differently. Next season, we start testing earlier, and we check the hardware before we check the code.
